    Curator mode

    Paste a YouTube link or video ID to add a song. Titles can be typed in the list, or left empty to be filled in from YouTube when the song plays. Your draft lives in this browser only; export it to bake it into the file.

    Add several at once

    One song per line. A line can be just “Artist – Title”, a YouTube link, or a title and a link separated by “|”. Lines without a link are kept as “needs a link”: use “Search on YouTube” next to them, then paste the link back into that row.
